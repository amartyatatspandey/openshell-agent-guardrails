03 — The ask, and how I scoped it

What you asked for, and the decisions I made

The ask
Show an AI agent inside an NVIDIA OpenShell sandbox trying things it shouldn't, and being denied, in ways that matter for Nirmata AIControls.

Deliverables: a git repo you can clone and run, and a video.


Not asked for, added as a labelled bonus:
Kyverno CEL deciding, OpenShell enforcing.
DecisionWhy
OpenShell core first, every control gradedIt's what you asked for, and it has to work on your machine the first time.
Kyverno bonus, separate and not gradedTies the demo to AIControls without putting the core at risk.
Deterministic, scripted attemptsSame result every run. A live LLM is nondeterministic and needs a key.
Fake data, reserved destinationsFAKE_NOT_A_REAL_TOKEN, example.com, .invalid, example-org/example-repo. Nothing real can leak.
Pin OpenShell v0.1.3, preflight firstIt's alpha. Your laptop should behave exactly like mine.
Grade from OpenShell's log, not stdoutThe sandbox is the witness, not my script.
