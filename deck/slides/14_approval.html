An approval can't take the agent past the boundary

Agent

Tries two unlisted hosts

example.com (outside the boundary) and raw.githubusercontent.com (inside it, but not in the agent's policy). Both are denied.

OpenShell 0.1.3

Proposes rules from the denials

Each comes back as raw L4 access for curl, at 65% confidence. Its own check reports "no new findings".

Approval gate

Composes and proves

Adds exactly what the proposal allows to the current policy, then runs openshell-prover against the signed boundary.

Decision

Rejected by proof, or a person decides

Outside → openshell rule reject, with the counterexample as the reason. Inside → y/N at a terminal. Never automatic.
